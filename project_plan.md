I. PROJECT DEFINITION

A. Overall Project Goal:
To launch a weekly blog series, “Sustainable Living Practices for Urban Dwellers,” aimed at increasing website engagement and positioning our company as a thought leader in sustainability among young professionals living in urban environments. The immediate objective is to plan the first month of content (4 blog posts), outlining each topic, the research and writing process, and a strategic promotional plan—all within an informative, actionable, and inspiring tone.

B. Core Deliverables:
1. First month’s editorial calendar (detailed topics/titles for four blog posts).
2. Research briefs for each blog post.
3. Draft outlines for each blog post.
4. A comprehensive promotional strategy outlining distribution channels and campaign tactics.
5. Assignment of team roles and responsibilities for execution.

C. Key Success Metrics:
- Completion and approval of four ready-to-execute post plans (including topics, research, and outlines) and a promotional plan by the deadline.
- Internal team sign-off indicating all stakeholders are aligned on scope and direction.
- Clear, actionable content and promotional steps laid out for seamless production and launch.

---

II. DETAILED EXECUTION PHASES & TASKS

Phase 1: Initiation & Research

- Phase Objective: Extract project requirements, align the team, and gather relevant research and inspiration for blog topics aligned with our goals and target audience.

  1. Task 1.1: Project Kickoff & Team Alignment
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Schedule kickoff meeting with research and content team.
       b. Review project goals, timeline, target audience, deliverables, and desired tone.
       c. Clarify individual roles and expectations.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Kickoff meeting minutes summarizing agreements.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 2 hours
     - Required Resources/Skills: Project management, facilitation.
     - Dependencies: None

  2. Task 1.2: Audience & Competitor Research
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Profile target audience (pain points, interests, content consumption habits).
       b. Analyze competitor and best-in-class sustainability blog series.
       c. Identify gaps and opportunities for unique positioning.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Target audience persona and research summary report.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 1 day
     - Required Resources/Skills: Research, analytical thinking.
     - Dependencies: Completion of kickoff meeting.

  3. Task 1.3: Brainstorm & Select Blog Post Topics
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Host brainstorming session with team to ideate at least 8 potential post topics.
       b. Analyze options for appeal, relevance, and alignment with audience needs and project goal.
       c. Select and prioritize four topics for the first month.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Documented shortlist with rationales for the 4 selected topics.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 4 hours
     - Required Resources/Skills: Team collaboration, subject knowledge.
     - Dependencies: Completion of audience and market research.

- Overall Phase Gate/Deliverable: Signed-off list of four blog post topics and corresponding audience research summary.
- Estimated Phase Duration: 3 days

---

Phase 2: Content Planning & Outlining

- Phase Objective: Develop detailed research briefs and content outlines for each selected blog post.

  1. Task 2.1: Develop Research Briefs for Each Topic
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Assign each topic to individual researchers/writers.
       b. For each topic, identify 3-5 authoritative sources, local relevance, actionable advice, and engaging stories or statistics.
       c. Summarize findings in a research brief format.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Four research briefs (one for each post).
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 1 day per post/topic (can run concurrently).
     - Required Resources/Skills: Research, content analysis.
     - Dependencies: Finalized post topics (Phase 1).

  2. Task 2.2: Create Detailed Post Outlines
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Translate each research brief into a structured outline (headline, key sections, main points, actionable tips, calls-to-action, tone/style notes).
       b. Circulate outlines for peer/team review and incorporate feedback.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Four detailed post outlines.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 2 hours per outline.
     - Required Resources/Skills: Content writing, editorial planning.
     - Dependencies: Completion of research briefs.

- Overall Phase Gate/Deliverable: Reviewed and approved outlines for all four posts, ready for drafting.
- Estimated Phase Duration: 3 days

---

Phase 3: Promotional Strategy Planning

- Phase Objective: Define the best-fit strategy and tactics for promoting the new blog series to maximize reach and engagement.

  1. Task 3.1: Audit Existing Promotional Channels
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. List and evaluate all channels available (company social media, email newsletter, website banners, partnerships).
       b. Review previous campaign analytics for reference.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Promotional channel audit brief.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 0.5 day
     - Required Resources/Skills: Marketing, analytics.
     - Dependencies: None

  2. Task 3.2: Develop Promotional Plan for Blog Launch
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Based on the audit, define a week-by-week promotional schedule.
       b. Specify creative needs per channel (e.g., visuals, microcopy).
       c. Assign tasks for copywriting, graphic, and publishing workflow.
       d. Outline KPIs (e.g., reach, engagement, blog traffic uplift).
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Comprehensive promotional plan (schedule, tactics, roles, KPIs).
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 1 day
     - Required Resources/Skills: Marketing planning, campaign expertise.
     - Dependencies: Channel audit.

- Overall Phase Gate/Deliverable: Finalized and team-approved promotional strategy and action plan.
- Estimated Phase Duration: 1.5 days

---

Phase 4: Internal Review & Handover

- Phase Objective: Ensure all deliverables are cohesive, high-quality, and actionable, with clear execution instructions for the production/launch phase.

  1. Task 4.1: Internal Review & Feedback
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Consolidate all deliverables (editorial calendar, research briefs, outlines, promotional plan).
       b. Distribute to key project stakeholders for final review.
       c. Gather feedback, make revisions as required.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Feedback log, final revised deliverables.
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 1 day
     - Required Resources/Skills: Editorial, project management.
     - Dependencies: All previous phase outputs.

  2. Task 4.2: Handover & Next Steps Planning
     - Actionable Steps:
       a. Package all finalized documents into a handover packet.
       b. Brief production/writing/publishing team on next steps.
       c. Confirm timeline for content drafting and go-live.
     - Task-Specific Deliverable(s): Complete campaign packet; action items for launch phase
     - Estimated Effort/Duration: 0.5 day
     - Required Resources/Skills: Communication, documentation.
     - Dependencies: Completion of review.

- Overall Phase Gate/Deliverable: Project packet with approved blog series plan and promotional strategy, ready for production.
- Estimated Phase Duration: 1.5 days

IV. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES (High-Level)

A. Suggested Key Roles:
  1. Project Lead/Manager
  2. Content Researchers
  3. Content Writers/Editors
  4. Marketing/Promotions Lead
  5. Graphic Designer (if needed for promo assets)
  6. Stakeholder Reviewer

B. Primary Responsibilities for Each Role:
  1. Project Lead/Manager: Oversees timeline, coordinates team, schedules meetings, ensures deliverables are completed and approved.
  2. Content Researchers: Gather data, stats, stories, and source material for each topic.
  3. Content Writers/Editors: Draft outlines, write clear and actionable sections, ensure tone fits project brief, incorporate feedback.
  4. Marketing/Promotions Lead: Audits channels, crafts promotional plan, coordinates with graphics/creative, analyzes channel performance.
  5. Graphic Designer: Designs supporting visuals for blog and promotional use.
  6. Stakeholder Reviewer: Provides project oversight, gives input on deliverables, ensures alignment with brand vision.

V. RISK IDENTIFICATION & MITIGATION

A. Potential Risks:
  1. Ambiguity around target audience results in poorly aligned content.
  2. Delays in research deliverables impact overall schedule.
  3. Promotional plan does not reach target demographic effectively.
  4. Lack of internal alignment slows approval/review process.

B. Proactive Mitigation Actions:
  1. Early and thorough audience research (addressed in Phase 1.2), periodic stakeholder check-ins.
  2. Assign back-up researcher/writer; build in daily progress standups; set mini-deadlines per task.
  3. Use audience data to inform channel/tactic selection; plan for A/B tests or pilot announcements.
  4. Schedule review sessions ahead of time with reminders; document all feedback and circulate revised versions rapidly.

---

VI. QUALITY ASSURANCE POINTS

A. Suggested Review & Quality Activities:
  - After Phase 1: Confirm audience research and chosen topics directly address company objectives and core demographic.
  - After Phase 2: Spot-check each outline to ensure posts are informative, actionable, and inspiring.
  - After Phase 3: Ensure promotional plan includes clear KPIs and a process for measuring effectiveness.
  - Final Deliverable: One final team review of the complete packet to check for cohesion, clarity, and actionable next steps.
